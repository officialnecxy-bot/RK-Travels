Cancellation & Refund Policy

We strive to offer fair and transparent cancellation terms.

Cancellation by Customer

  • More than 24 hours before trip: Full refund (minus ₹50 processing fee).
  • 12 to 24 hours before trip: 50% refund.
  • Less than 12 hours or no‑show: No refund.

Cancellation by RK TRAVELS

If we cancel due to vehicle breakdown, extreme weather, or other unavoidable circumstances, we offer:

  • Full refund + 10% future travel credit, OR
  • Alternative vehicle (if available).

Refund Process

  • Online payments: Refund initiated within 3‑5 business days to original source.
  • Offline bookings: Cash/UPI refund on the spot.
  • All refunds are credited within 7‑10 days depending on your bank.

Modification

Date/time changes are treated as cancellation + new booking. However, you may modify the booking once free of charge if requested at least 12 hours prior.

Special Trips (Outstation, Round Trip)

Cancellation charges for outstation journeys may be higher due to driver deadhead. Please check your booking confirmation for specific terms.
